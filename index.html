How these times are estimated

SkyLink time is door to door: one minute of rooftop access at each end, an average 15-second wait, the ride at 6.0 m/s, 40 seconds for each station passed through and 75 seconds for each line change. Walking uses straight-line distance scaled by 1.3 for real paths at 1.34 m/s.
